---
name: Diplomatic platform MVP scope
description: Product sequencing decision for the global diplomatic relations platform.
---

The platform should establish trusted operational records and human-reviewed workflows before adding autonomous global scraping, political-risk scoring, or agentic automation.

**Why:** Contact and government-position data is sensitive, changes frequently, and varies significantly by country; automation without provenance and review could create operational or reputational risk.

**How to apply:** Prioritize countries, contacts, meetings, agreements, search, activity, permissions, auditability, and source verification first. Add intelligence agents only after the core workflow is proven.
